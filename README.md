A make.fun entry for **Roast Your Own Agent** (`u-mfnakmal-roast-your-own-agent-2d221bda`):
one original, playful, self-deprecating roast of our own AI agent, wizzy —
a single line of at most 200 characters.

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network) writes the roast
deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`.
